Problem Statement:
People want to stay fit. Most of them fail. Not because they lack motivation, but because they
lack the right support at the right time.
The fitness industry is not short on solutions. There are gym memberships, personal trainers,
YouTube channels, diet plans, and hundreds of apps on the app store. But most people still
struggle. The gym is too expensive for regular use. A personal trainer is a luxury most cannot
afford. And the apps that exist today either fall too short or overwhelm the user entirely.
The result is a familiar pattern. Someone sets a goal, starts strong, loses track after a few weeks,
and eventually stops. The problem is not willpower. It is the absence of a reliable, affordable
system that keeps someone on track without requiring them to already be a fitness expert. That is
the gap this project addresses.
Objectives:
1. Unified Activity Tracking:Users can log workouts, steps, calories, and rest days all in one
place. The data builds over time into a clear picture of their progress.
2. Personalized Recommendations: The app looks at the user's fitness level, goals, and history,
then generates workout and nutrition guidance that actually matches where they are. Generic
plans do not work. This one adapts.
3. Consistency and Accountability: Progress charts, milestones, and smart reminders help users
stay on track. Small wins build momentum and make it easier to keep going than to quit.
4. Accessible Coaching:Not everyone can afford a trainer. The app brings structured coaching
logic to beginners, students, and users with limited access to fitness facilities.
5. Long-Term Usability: Simple on day one, still relevant a year later. As the user grows, the
app grows with them.

Competitor Analysis:
Researching on the internet, two of the biggest competitors are as follows:
Competitor 1: MyFitnessPal
Introduction:
MyFitnessPal is a nutrition-focused fitness application designed primarily to help
users track their daily food intake and calorie consumption. It is widely used by
people who want to lose weight, gain weight, or maintain a healthy diet.
Figure 1:
Main Features:
• Food & Calorie Tracking
Users can search from a massive food database or scan barcodes to quickly log meals.
This is the app’s strongest feature.
• Nutritional Breakdown
Provides detailed insights into protein, fat, and carbohydrate intake, helping users balance
their diet.
• Goal Setting
Users set goals, and the app calculates daily calorie limits.
• Progress Monitoring
Tracks weight changes, calorie trends, and consistency over time using graphs.
• Exercise Logging
Users can manually add workouts, but it is not the app’s main strength.
Overall Insight:
MyFitnessPal is excellent for diet management, however lacks:
• Deep workout guidance
• Personalized coaching
• Smart fitness planning
Competitor 2: Nike Training Club
Nike Training Club is a workout focused app that provides users with guided exercise routines
designed by professional trainers.
Figure 2:
Users select a workout or training plan based on:
• Fitness level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
• Goals (strength, endurance, flexibility)
The app then provides guided workouts, often with videos.
Main Features:
• Guided Workouts
High-quality video demonstrations help users perform exercises correctly.
• Structured Training Programs
multi-week plans designed to achieve specific goals like weight loss or muscle gain.
• Workout Scheduling
Helps users stay consistent by organizing workouts over time.
• Minimal Equipment Options
Includes home workouts that require little or no equipment.
• Motivational Design
Clean UI engages users.
Overall Insight:
Nike Training Club excels in workout guidance, but lacks:
• Detailed calorie/diet tracking
• Advanced progress analytics
• Personalized adaptive coaching
Identifying The Gap:
Neither app provides a fully integrated solution that includes:
• Diet tracking
• Workout planning
• Personalized coaching
• Real-time analytics
So we develop an app that combines all of these into a single one.
